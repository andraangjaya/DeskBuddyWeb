Help (FAQ)

Pomodoro Timer

1. What is a session?
A session is one focused work or study period, usually set to 25 minutes. After each session, you take a short 5 minutes rest before starting the next one.
2. How is distraction time counted?
When a distraction is detected (you leave your desk, use your handphone, etc.), the session timer pauses. The time spent distracted is recorded in your Distraction History.
3. Does distraction time reduce my 25 minutes session?
No. Your 25-minute session only counts active focus time. Distraction time is paused and the timer resumes after the distraction ends.
4. What is the difference between Session History and Distraction History?
• Session History, Shows start and end time of every work and rest period.
• Distraction History, Shows each distraction's start time, end time, and duration.
5. Can I change the session length?
No. Your session length is fixed at 25 minutes due to pomodoro method

General

1. Do you track my activity outside this website?
No. We only track activity related to your timer usage for performance stats.
2. Can I change the theme or appearance?
Yes. Go to Settings → Appearance to switch between light and dark themes
3. Is there a premium or paid version?
Currently, all features are free. A premium plan may be introduced in the future for advanced tracking and customization.
4. Can I adjust the alarm sound?
No. The alarm sound is fixed and cannot be changed to ensure a consistent studying experience.
